Highlights of Wüest Dimensions

Valuation

Valuation combines various models and functionalities for efficient and detailed valuation of all types of properties in your portfolio.

The methods take into account established standards and include the following models: discounted cash flow (DCF), residual value (RES), capitalization (CAP), hedonic models (HED), etc.

Learn more

Market Data Analysis

Comprehensive, detailed geo-referenced real estate market analyses, that's what Market Data Analysis offers. Current and historical data on real estate markets as well as socio-demographic data enable far-sighted analyses and differentiated ratings of locations.

The integrated, intuitively usable functionalities enable a targeted use of the comprehensive data pools.

Learn more

Investment Management

Experience professional, comprehensive real estate investment management on a single platform. Access the reliable database you need for sustainable value creation. Ensure targeted portfolio management and optimal performance while planning individual component renovations and their associated investments, all within the platform.

Enable comprehensive planning with detailed status analyses, investment and innovative life cycle planning, and interactive before-and-after scenarios. AI-driven renovation proposals combine these measures strategically, tailored to your specific risk appetite.

Learn more

Process Management

Comprehensive process management for the administration of object and stakeholder data. Intelligent, value-oriented for highest quality requirements and efficient collaboration.

Achieve more sustainable and higher returns, as well as lower costs and risks. All aspects of the entire value chain united on one platform.
